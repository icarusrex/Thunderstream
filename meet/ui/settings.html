Google Meet for invitations

Create a fresh Meet link from the configured Google account before a new invitation draft opens. Your calendar and OpenADR organizer stay selected.

Google receives a meeting-creation request. Your invitation title, time, description and guests stay in Thunderbird.

Checking connection…

Edits keep their existing link. Cancelling a draft can leave an unused meeting link. If creation fails, the draft opens with an explanation before you send.

This preview supports Mac Thunderbird 157.0.1, build 20261001134409.
